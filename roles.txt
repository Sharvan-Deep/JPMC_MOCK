1. Admin

The Admin manages the system itself:

Admin
├── Manage Jaldhaara staff accounts
├── Manage roles/access
├── Manage company/data ingestion settings
├── Monitor system/data status
└── View overall system activity
2. Fundraising Staff

The Fundraising Staff is the primary operational user:

Fundraising Staff
├── Search companies
├── Filter companies
├── View company details
├── View CSR information/history
├── View AI priority/result
├── Review AI reasoning/recommendations
├── Select potential donor leads
└── Manage donor outreach/leads
